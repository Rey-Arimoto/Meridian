# Meridian Alpha: Intelligence Against Entropy

## What is Alpha in Meridian?

Meridian’s alpha does not come from predicting price direction better than others.

It comes from **correctly identifying when the market is no longer explainable by existing assumptions — and acting accordingly**.

In other words:

> **Meridian extracts alpha from entropy.**

---

## Core Idea

Markets are usually treated as if they are always interpretable:
- trends exist,
- signals are meaningful,
- risk models remain valid.

This assumption fails precisely when the largest losses occur.

Meridian introduces **Entropy Regimes** to explicitly model *when* the market stops making sense.

Alpha emerges from **doing less when explanation breaks down**, and **acting earlier when order reforms**.

---

## Entropy Regime Definition

An **Entropy Regime** describes how explainable the current market state is relative to existing rules, strategies, and assumptions.

| Regime | Description |
|---|---|
| **Stable Regime** | Low entropy. Market behavior aligns with historical assumptions. |
| **Unstable Regime** | Entropy rising. Assumptions begin to fail. |
| **Chaotic Regime** | High entropy. Market behavior is no longer explainable. |
| **Reformation Regime** | Entropy high but declining. New order is forming. |

---

## Entropy Regime × Action Matrix (Alpha Engine)

| Entropy Regime | Market Condition | Meridian Action | Alpha Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable | Predictable, low entropy | Maintain baseline exposure | Trend / carry |
| Unstable | Entropy rising | Reduce exposure / NoOp | Avoid false signals |
| Chaotic | High entropy | **Freeze / NoOp** | Loss avoidance premium |
| Reformation | Entropy falling | Gradual re-entry | Early regime capture |

---

## The NoOp Premium

In traditional systems, *not trading* is treated as absence of action.

In Meridian, **NoOp is a first-class strategic action**.

During Chaotic Regimes:
- Most systems are forced to act,
- Signals are unreliable,
- Slippage and correlation spike.

Meridian’s alpha here is **negative alpha avoidance**:
> avoiding losses that others cannot avoid.

This avoidance compounds over time and becomes a dominant performance driver.
